# Agri-Slot Site Roadmap & Vision (SITE.md)

## 1. Project Overview
Agri-Slot is an advanced mobile-first smart agriculture slot booking and farm management application designed for modern farmers in South Asia.

## 2. Core Screen Structure
- **Screen 1: Home & Hero (`index.html#home`)** — Weather telemetry, slot booking widget, seasonal crops slider, quick actions.
- **Screen 2: Farmer Dashboard (`index.html#dashboard`)** — Acreage stats, crop stage progress, upcoming slot tasks, field activity log.
- **Screen 3: Crop Details & Management (`index.html#crop-details`)** — 5-stage agronomy growth cycle, task scheduler, micro-weather advisories, input log.
- **Screen 4: Saved / Managed Farm (`index.html#managed-farm`)** — Field grid layout, slot health status, active equipment reservations.

## 3. Auxiliary Modules
- **KisanAI Assistant**: Floating agronomic chatbot with instant advice.
- **Slot Reservation Engine**: Equipment, drone spraying, irrigation pump, and cold storage booking.
- **Multilingual Support**: Dynamic switching across EN, TE, HI, TA, KN.
- **Analytics & Yield Predictor**: Financial & crop yield visualization.
